Marketing roadmap

Three tiers. The middle one has to work.

Local pages recruit creators. The Creator Network connects them with businesses. Large cases are Jamie and Heath's own work. The network in the middle is what makes the business work.

How the tiers connect

Ads bring businesses into the network, and local pages bring creators. The biggest businesses move up to large cases.

Tier 2 · The coreCreator Network

This is where businesses and creators connect through our platform. Both sides pay a subscription. It's at Stage 1 of the roadmap: no members and no proof yet. So prove it in Central Arkansas and Dallas before going anywhere else.

Assumes an 85% margin and the roadmap's rule of gross profit at 3:1 over acquisition cost. One business member is worth about 13 creator members: creators bring the supply, businesses bring the revenue.

  • Launch the smallest version people will pay for: creator pages, a brand-logo wall, open briefs, and a business subscription that posts briefs. Swipe matching comes next.
  • Have briefs waiting before creators arrive. Creators pay because briefs pay out. Get the first paying businesses from ads and from the local pages' business contacts.
  • Founding members on both sides: an early rate in exchange for a testimonial, a published result and permission to use them in ads.
  • Quick win in the first 48 hours: a creator's page goes live and they see their first brief; a business gets its first creator match.
  • Weekly numbers: paying businesses, paying creators, briefs posted, briefs filled, cancellations.

Tier 3Local content pages recruit creators

atLocal Central Arkansas, atLocal Dallas and the local food pages are how creators find us. Every post shows what being in the network looks like.

  • Feature local creators in every post, tagged, with their atLocal page linked. Being featured is the reason to join.
  • Every page has one call to action for creators: "Join the atLocal Creator Network."
  • Film from what's already happening: local spots, events and client shoots. Behind-the-scenes footage also becomes ad creative.
  • The businesses we feature are network leads: follow up with each one about a business subscription.
  • Track creator sign-ups by page so we know which page and city recruit best.

Tier 1Large cases: Jamie + Heath only

Kept separate from the rest of the business. At most 6 cases at once, and Jamie and Heath each need $50K a month. 90-day test: if it doesn't happen, we raise money.

  • Slots: one is active and one is onboarding, so 4 are open. Each case needs to average about $16.7K a month.
  • About 14 qualified calls closes 4 cases, at the roadmap's ~30% close rate for in-person sales.
  • Get paid at signing. Above a 60% close rate means we're underpriced.

Don't do yet

  • Expanding past Central Arkansas and Dallas before the network fills both.
  • Building every feature before briefs are being posted and filled.
  • Paid ads for creators while local pages recruit them for less.
  • Judging any tier by views instead of paying members and signed cases.

The one move

Get the Creator Network working in Central Arkansas: businesses from ads, creators from the local pages, and briefs filled every week.

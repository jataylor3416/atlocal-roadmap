Business + marketing plan

Large cases fund us. The Creator Network is the big deal.

Jamie and Heath run the large cases separately. Everything else runs on @atlocal.media ads: ads send people to our products, and the Creator Network sits in the middle, connecting businesses and creators who also collab on our local pages.

How it fits

Businesses come in through ads and a product, then subscribe to match with creators. Creators subscribe to match with businesses and collab with us on the local pages.

SeparateLarge cases: Jamie + Heath

Jamie brings in the client, Heath joins the call, and we close and do the work. The goal is $50K a month each, $100K combined, from 6 cases at most. That means $300K over the 90-day test.

  • Every product buyer gets offered a business subscription, so a product sale turns into a monthly member.
  • Have briefs waiting before creators arrive. Creators pay because there are businesses to match with.
  • Launch the smallest version people will pay for: creator pages, a brand-logo wall, open briefs and matching. Swipe-style comes next.

  • Weekly numbers: paying businesses, paying creators, matches made, cancellations.

CollabLocal pages: atLocal Central Arkansas + atLocal Dallas

The local pages are where we collab with network creators. That gives creators a reason to join and gives the network content people can see.

  • Collab with network creators on every local post, tagged, with their atLocal page linked.
  • Each page has one call to action for creators: join the atLocal Creator Network.
  • Local businesses we feature are leads for a product or a network subscription.
  • Track creator sign-ups by page and city.

Don't do yet

  • Using ads to chase large cases. Those come from Jamie.
  • Expanding the network past Central Arkansas and Dallas before both are full.
  • Building every feature before matches are happening.
  • Judging anything by views instead of sales, subscriptions and signed cases.

The one move

Price the large cases at $50K and fill all 6. Meanwhile, ads sell products, and every product buyer is offered a Creator Network subscription.

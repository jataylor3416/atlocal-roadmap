1The math

With 2 of 6 slots taken, we need 4 more large cases, and each case has to average about $16.7K a month.

SlotStatus
1 · Foundation programActive
2 · Orthopedic networkOnboarding now
3 – 6Open: close 4 in 90 days
  • Price every case to about $16.7K a month or more, as a retainer or a build fee spread over the months of delivery. A one-time fee alone can't keep a slot paying every month.
  • Work out how many calls we need. The roadmap's benchmark for in-person consultative sales is about a 30% close rate. That means about 14 qualified calls to close 4 cases, or 1 to 2 a week.
  • Check close rate after every 5 calls. Above 60% means we're underpriced. Below 30% means fix the pitch before buying more calls.
  • Get paid at signing, as with the current cases.

2Ads drive everything

@atlocal.media ads have one job: book qualified consultations with businesses that can fill a slot.

  • Every ad points to a booked consultation or the AI Answer Check (a one-page report on what AI says about them). Never "request a quote."
  • Make the booking form qualify: ask about budget, decision-maker, need and timing, so only businesses that can fill a slot get a call.
  • Have Meta optimize for booked calls, not clicks or opt-ins.
  • Test, then scale: one angle and one format per ad set. Turn off any ad whose cost passes 3x the target cost per qualified call. Split the budget 70% on winners, 30% on new tests.
  • Test budget: be willing to spend about one case's first month (~$16.7K) over the 90 days to find what works. That's the roadmap's "minimum ante."
  • Every Monday: qualified calls booked, cost per call, and slots filled. Are they normal?
  • Never promise placement in AI answers.
